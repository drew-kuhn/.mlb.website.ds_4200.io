2024 Home Run Leaders

Home runs are one of the easiest baseball statistics to understand: the batter hits the ball over the fence and the play scores at least one run. Home runs have become more and more important to ranking players as statistics have entered the game. Some analysts argue that home runs are the most impactful statistic in baseball. Home runs alone are not enough to compare all hitters, because a total depends on playing time, health, ballpark, and lineup position. The chart below shows five of the league's top home run hitters in 2024 instead of trying to rank everyone.

Aaron Judge led this group with 58 home runs, and Shohei Ohtani finished close behind with 54. Anthony Santander hit 44, Juan Soto hit 41, and Jose Ramirez hit 39. These are some of the most prolific home run hitters in the league. Aaron Judge and Shohei Ohtani both won their league's MVP awards in the 2024 season. The chart is very good at making differences clear between players. We have to remember that a gap of a few home runs says nothing about walks, defense, baserunning, or when each home run happened. These can be just as important for how valuable an MLB player is.

Ranking by home runs

  1. Aaron Judge: 58
  2. Shohei Ohtani: 54
  3. Anthony Santander: 44
  4. Juan Soto: 41
  5. Jose Ramirez: 39

Three tips for reading the chart

  • Compare the overall range before focusing on one player.
  • Remember these are season totals, not rates per plate appearance.
  • Use the chart as a starting point to look into each player's season.

Source: MLB.com 2024 regular-season statistics.
