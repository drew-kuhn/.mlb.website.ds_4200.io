Welcome to My MLB Website

Hi, I'm Drew, and in this website I will be talking about a sport I love: baseball. Baseball was the first sport I played as a kid and it's one of the sports I played in high school. I'm a big Philadelphia Phillies fan, and I've been following the sport each and every year for the past decade.

The length of the season also makes baseball feel like a running conversation. Baseball is a sport that relies on a heavy amount of data and is commonly seen as a sport that uses data science. I would even credit baseball for being one of the reasons I took an interest in data and data science in general. This is why when given the chance I always look to baseball as a way to understand data science better.

I made this website for my DS 4200 class to explore the combination of baseball and data visualization. The other page highlights a D3 bar chart of 2024 home run totals for five of the league's best power hitters. I wanted to learn D3 by using stats for a sport that I already care about. I hope you enjoy it and I've enjoyed getting more familiar with HTML and CSS (although it is very difficult).
